Delete Your Data

If you would like to delete your Fetch account and all associated data, you can do so by following these steps:

Option 1: Delete from the App

  1. Open the Fetch app
  2. Go to the Profile tab
  3. Tap Delete Account
  4. Confirm the deletion

This will permanently delete your account, preferences, swipe history, and all matches.

Option 2: Request Deletion by Email

Send an email to privacy@llmgames.org with the subject line "Delete My Account" and include the email address associated with your Fetch account. We will process your request within 30 days.

What Gets Deleted

  • Your account and profile information
  • Your dog preferences
  • Your swipe history (likes and passes)
  • Your matches
  • Your notification settings

Facebook Data

If you signed in with Facebook, deleting your Fetch account removes all data we received from Facebook. To also revoke Fetch's access to your Facebook account, go to Facebook Settings > Apps and Websites and remove Fetch.

For questions, contact privacy@llmgames.org.

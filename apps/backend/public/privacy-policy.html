Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 31, 2026

Fetch ("we", "our", or "the app") is a dog adoption matching application that connects users with rescue dogs available for adoption and fostering. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information.

1. Information We Collect

Account Information

When you create an account, we collect:

  • Email address - used for account authentication and communication
  • Display name - shown in your profile
  • Profile photo - if provided through your account or OAuth provider

OAuth Login

If you sign in with Google or Facebook, we receive your basic profile information (name, email, and profile picture) from those services. We do not access your contacts, posts, or other social media data.

Usage Data

We collect information about how you use the app, including:

  • Dog preferences you set (size, breed, energy level, etc.)
  • Swipe history (likes and passes on dog profiles)
  • Match and adoption inquiry data

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Provide and maintain the Fetch app
  • Calculate compatibility scores between you and available dogs
  • Show you personalized dog recommendations based on your preferences
  • Facilitate connections with rescue organizations for adoption or fostering
  • Send notifications about matches and updates (with your consent)
  • Improve the app experience

3. Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored securely using Supabase (PostgreSQL database with row-level security). Authentication tokens are encrypted and stored securely on your device. We use HTTPS for all data transmission.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We may share limited information with:

  • Rescue organizations - when you express interest in adopting or fostering a dog, we share your contact information with the relevant organization
  • Service providers - we use Supabase for database hosting and Expo for push notifications, which process data on our behalf

5. Third-Party Services

The app integrates with the following third-party services:

  • Supabase - authentication and database (Supabase Privacy Policy)
  • Google Sign-In - optional OAuth login (Google Privacy Policy)
  • Facebook Login - optional OAuth login (Meta Privacy Policy)
  • Expo - push notifications (Expo Privacy Policy)

6. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access your personal data
  • Update your profile information through the app
  • Delete your account and associated data by contacting us
  • Opt out of push notifications through your device settings

7. Children's Privacy

Fetch is not intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new policy on this page and updating the "Last updated" date.
